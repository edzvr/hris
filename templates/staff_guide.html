Staff Guide

Use this page as a quick guide for your daily work, records, quiz, and evaluation tasks.

Daily Attendance

  • Clock in when you start work and clock out when you finish.
  • 8:00 AM to 8:10 AM is Present. 8:11 AM onwards is Late.
  • Check My Attendance to review your recorded time and hours.
  • If there is a missed or wrong time, submit an Attendance Correction request with the correct time and reason.
Open My Attendance Request Correction

Records To Download

  • Download your Attendance Record regularly for your personal copy.
  • Download your Payslip after Admin finalizes it for the Saturday salary release.
  • The company keeps records online to reduce paper use. Keep your downloaded copies secure.
Download Attendance PDF Open My Payslips

Quiz And Assessment

  • Open Take Quiz when a quiz is available.
  • Review the question and submit your answer before leaving the page.
  • A score of 75% or higher earns merit points. A lower score adds demerit points under the current system rules.
  • Use Assessments to check available assessment activities and results.
Take Quiz Open Assessments

Monthly Peer Evaluation

  • Complete a fair peer evaluation for each coworker once every calendar month.
  • Give ratings based on actual work performance, teamwork, and conduct.
  • Submit before the end of the month so the evaluation is included in the monthly review.
  • Do not evaluate yourself.
Open Peer Evaluation

Merit And Demerit

  • Merit points recognize good attendance, passing quiz scores, and evaluation results.
  • Perfect recorded attendance for the month gives 10 merit points.
  • Each Late record adds 2 demerit points; each Absent record adds 5 demerit points.
  • Use the page to review your current points and understand the result.
View Merit / Demerit

Other Pages

  • My Payroll: view finalized payslips and salary records.
  • Apply Leave and Apply Loan: submit requests for Admin review.
  • Company Files: download documents shared with your company.
  • My Profile: keep your contact and personal information updated.
