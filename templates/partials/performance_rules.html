Merit / demerit rules and reporting limitations
  • Attendance: Present +1 merit; recorded Absent +1 demerit, once per date. Approved leave is excluded from absence penalties. Missing logs are not inferred absences. Late, undertime, and OT do not add new points or penalties.
  • Approved admin evaluation monthly average per evaluator: at least 4.5 = +5 merit; at least 3.5 = +3 merit; below 1.5 = +5 demerit; below 2.5 = +3 demerit.
  • Approved peer evaluation monthly average per evaluator: at least 4.5 = +2 merit; at least 3.5 = +1 merit; below 1.5 = +2 demerit; below 2.5 = +1 demerit. Other averages are neutral. Only approved, identified admin/peer evaluations with valid 1–5 ratings count.
  • Date-filtered evaluation groups include all approved monthly answers and are attributed to the last approved answer's submission date, not the approval date.
  • Official quiz: score / total points at least 75% = +5 merit. Practice attempts do not count; failures have no automatic demerit.
  • Other recorded awards use the existing ledger, excluding evaluation entries already represented above. Legacy awards without a source retain their recorded recipient; their origin has not been reconstructed.
  • Reporting totals are not stored point balances or redeemable cash. Historical balances, redemption history, and the legacy redemption workflow are unchanged.
  • No overall performance rating, employee ranking, or automatic employment recommendation is generated.
