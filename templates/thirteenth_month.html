13th-Month Pay

Computed from prorated basic salary earned during the selected calendar year, divided by 12.

Unpaid absences, late time, half-day, undertime, and holiday without pay reduce the 13th-month basis only when they reduce the employee's earned basic pay; they are not deducted again from the final 13th-month amount. Overtime, allowances, incentives, and statutory or loan deductions are excluded from the basis.
