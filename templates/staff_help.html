Customize your dashboard

  1. Open My Account > Settings in the sidebar. On mobile, open Staff menu first.
  2. Choose a color under Buttons & sidebar color, then click Save Settings. Custom colors apply to both buttons and sidebar, with readable text automatically selected.
  3. You can also change sidebar text size, button size, and buttons per row.
  4. Use Reset Layout to restore the soft cream-yellow sidebar, muted blue buttons, light gray background, and default layout. HR records are not deleted.

Settings stay saved on this browser/device only. Red action buttons such as Clock Out keep their warning color.
