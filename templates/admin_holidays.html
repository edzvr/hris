Holidays

Payroll detects these dates automatically. Worked Special Non-Working days are paid 130% (150% on rest day). Worked Regular Holidays are paid 200% (260% on rest day). An unworked Regular Holiday is paid 100% when the staff actually worked (completed Clock In/Out) on the workday before and after it; paid leave does not qualify. Official national holidays are added automatically; add Eid and local holidays here when proclaimed. Saving an existing date updates it.
